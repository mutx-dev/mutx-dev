# Governed runtime benchmark results

Generated by `run_benchmark.py`. Decisions are deterministic and reproduce exactly;
latencies depend on the machine listed at the bottom.

## Headline

Of 451 recorded prompt-injection attacks that succeeded against an undefended agent,
the policy held or blocked at least one attacker-goal call in 408 (90.5%,
95% CI Wilson 87.4% to 92.8%, task-bootstrap 86.9% to 94.1%).
The price: 197 of 388 benign tasks (50.8%,
95% CI Wilson 45.8% to 55.7%, task-bootstrap 42.3% to 60.1%) contained a call that needed approval.

The policy decides on tool names only. It cannot tell an injected `send_email` from a
legitimate one, so the interception rate and the approval load come from the same gate.
